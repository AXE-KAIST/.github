## About

**AXE Lab** is the **Automotive Intelligence × Electrification Lab** at **KAIST**, working at the intersection of autonomous driving intelligence, vehicle electrification, mobility AI, and real-vehicle validation.

Our research focuses on building intelligent vehicle systems that can perceive, predict, plan, learn, and operate safely in complex real-world driving environments, while also advancing efficient electric and hybrid powertrain technologies for future mobility.

---

## Research Areas

### Autonomous Vehicles

We study AI systems for autonomous driving, including:

- End-to-End autonomous driving
- World-model-based driving simulation
- Reinforcement learning for driving policy learning
- Sim-to-real transfer and real-vehicle validation
- Interactive motion planning
- Collision avoidance and mitigation
- Vehicle trajectory prediction
- 3D occupancy prediction
- Camera-radar fusion and robust perception
- Active/domain adaptation for scalable autonomous-driving perception
- Level 4 / Level 4+ autonomous-driving testbed environments

### Electric Powertrain

We study intelligent and efficient electric vehicle systems, including:

- Electric vehicle and hybrid electric vehicle powertrain design
- EREV energy-management and SOC planning
- Fuel-cell electric vehicle thermal-management modeling
- Vehicle motion prediction for energy optimization
- Optimal control for vehicle energy efficiency
- Data-driven powertrain control and planning

## Notes for Contributors

When contributing to AXE Lab repositories:

1. Keep code modular, documented, and reproducible.
2. Include clear installation and experiment-running instructions.
3. Add citation information when the repository supports a publication.
4. Avoid uploading private datasets, credentials, API keys, or internal documents.
5. Use issues and pull requests for transparent collaboration.
